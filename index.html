CONTROLS

  • Move: ↑↓←→ or WASD — on touch: swipe in the direction.
  • Pause: P or Esc (or the ⏸ button).
  • Help: H or the ? button · Audio: M · Confirm: Enter.
  • Camera: drag to rotate, scroll wheel to zoom — on touch: two fingers to rotate/zoom.

OBJECTIVE

Eat the crystals to grow and score points. Don't crash into your own body or the purple obstacles: with the power-up Ghost you pass through them. The grid borders are passable: exit on one side and re-enter on the other.

LEVELS AND SCORING

Every 300 points you level up (each next level costs +100): the game speeds up and spawns 3–6 obstacle blocks (more at higher levels). The record is saved on your device.
